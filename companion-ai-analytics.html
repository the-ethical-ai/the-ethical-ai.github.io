Reading the data

Source, scope & limitations

Source: Similarweb website traffic estimates, manually collected by Tyler Chang. Values represent monthly visits in millions, not unique visitors, accounts, or app usage.

This is a selection of prominent companion AI websites, not an exhaustive list of the market. Coverage expands over time; not all 20 platforms were tracked throughout the period. Missing observations remain unrecorded and are excluded from totals, means, and medians.

Totals describe the websites recorded in each month. Changes in these totals can reflect added coverage as well as traffic changes, so they should not be interpreted as market growth. The same-platform comparison uses only platforms recorded in both the first and latest months.

Summary statistics are calculated from the platform rows. Metrics and this visualization will be updated monthly as new observations are collected.
